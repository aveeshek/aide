# Graph Engineering Rules
1. Resolve graph-backed task context before cross-repository planning.
2. Use typed relationships to discover services, APIs, events, schemas, data stores, tests, owners, ADRs, and rollout dependencies.
3. Preserve source, commit, pointer, evidence class, review status, and validity for every claim.
4. Keep declared, contracted, implemented, and observed evidence distinct; surface drift and contradictions.
5. Use deterministic parsers for exact structures and LLMs only for synthesis or candidate relations.
6. Git-backed Markdown is canonical; Neo4j is rebuildable; Graphiti and OpenWiki are derived/advisory.
7. Kiro may propose a delta but may not publish without validation, CI, CODEOWNERS, and review.
